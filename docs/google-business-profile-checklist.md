#### Review Request Checklist:
- [ ] Only ask satisfied clients (never pressure)
- [ ] Send 1-2 weeks after final session or milestone
- [ ] Use HIPAA-compliant language (no specific treatment details)
- [ ] Provide direct Google review link
- [ ] Make it optional, not required

#### Respond to ALL Reviews:
- [ ] Thank reviewer within 24-48 hours
- [ ] Keep responses professional and brief
- [ ] Never discuss treatment details

**Goal:** 10+ reviews within first 90 days

## 🚨 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **DON'T:**
- Keyword stuff business name ("Healing Therapy Center Best ADHD Dearborn")
- Use PO Box as address
- List competitors in description
- Ask clients to mention specific services in reviews (HIPAA violation)
- Post promotional content only
- Ignore negative reviews
- Use stock photos (Google can detect these)
- Set incorrect business hours
- Change business name frequently

✅ **DO:**
- Keep NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistent across all directories
- Upload new photos monthly
- Post weekly updates
- Respond to all reviews within 48 hours
- Update special hours for holidays
- Monitor insights weekly
- Keep description under 750 characters
