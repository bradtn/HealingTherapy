Psychological Testing & Autism Evaluations

At Healing Therapy Center, we offer comprehensive psychological testing services designed to provide clarity, insight, and guidance for individuals of all ages. Our experienced psychologists use a range of standardized tests and evaluation tools to gather valuable insights into your cognitive, emotional, and behavioral functioning

What is Psychological Testing?

Psychological testing involves a series of assessments conducted by a licensed psychologist to evaluate different aspects of an individual’s mental health. These tests can help diagnose conditions such as anxiety, depression, Autism, ADHD, learning disabilities, and more. The results of these tests provide a detailed understanding of an individual’s strengths and areas for improvement, guiding effective treatment planning.

  • Career and Vocational Assessments
Who Can Benefit from Psychological Testing?
  • Children (Ages 2 and Up) – If your child is experiencing delays in speech, difficulty making friends, or struggles in school, an evaluation may uncover valuable insights.
  • Adolescents – Teenagers may experience anxiety, depression, or behavioral concerns as they navigate the pressures of school and social life. Testing can identify specific needs and offer targeted support.
  • Adults – For adults struggling with anxiety, executive functioning, or persistent interpersonal challenges, psychological testing can offer clarity on areas like ADHD, anxiety, or autism spectrum disorders.
Benefits of Psychological Testing

Whether you’re seeking an autism evaluation or need help understanding behavioral challenges, psychological testing can be transformative. Here’s how testing can help:

  • Clarification of Diagnosis: Identify conditions such as autism, ADHD, anxiety, and learning disabilities that may be impacting daily life.
  • Personalized Treatment Plans: Once a diagnosis is clear, you’ll receive specific recommendations and treatment options.
  • School Accommodations: Testing results can support 504 Plans, Individualized Education Programs (IEPs), and other academic accommodations.
  • Access to Resources: Get connected to resources like speech therapy, occupational therapy, social skills groups, and other support services.
