# Directory Listing Strategy (2026)

## Purpose
Directory listings are a 2026 SEO priority as brand signals that strengthen local search rankings and AI search citations. Each listing creates:
- Backlinks to your website
- NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency signals
- Brand mentions Google and AI engines track
- Direct referral traffic from people searching directories

## NAP Consistency (CRITICAL)

**Your NAP must be IDENTICAL across all listings:**

```
Name: Healing Therapy Center
Address: 22005 Outer Dr W, Dearborn, MI 48124
Phone: (313) 654-1915
Website: https://www.healingtherapycenter.com
Email: info@healingtherapycenter.com (if listed)
```

**Rules:**
- Exact same spelling, spacing, punctuation
- Same phone number format: (313) 654-1915
- Same address format (including "Outer Dr W" not "Outer Drive West")
- HTTPS version of website URL everywhere
- Don't abbreviate Dearborn as "Dbn" or "Dborn"

**Why It Matters:**
- Google uses NAP to verify business legitimacy
- Inconsistent NAP confuses search engines
- Can hurt local SEO rankings

## Review Management Strategy

### Requesting Reviews (CAREFULLY)

**DO:**
- Ask satisfied clients to review on Google or Facebook
- Send a follow-up email 1-2 weeks after successful treatment ends
- Make it easy (include direct links)
- Respond to all reviews (positive and negative)

**DON'T:**
- Ask for Yelp reviews (triggers filter)
- Incentivize reviews (violates policies)
- Ask during active treatment (ethical issue)
- Only ask happy clients (looks fake)

**Email Template:**
```
Subject: How was your experience at Healing Therapy Center?

Hi [Name],

We hope you're doing well! If you found our services helpful, we'd be grateful if you'd share your experience online. Reviews help other people in our community find the support they need.

You can leave a review here:
- Google: [direct link to Google review form]
- Facebook: [direct link to Facebook reviews]

Thank you for trusting us with your care.

Best,
Healing Therapy Center Team
```

## Red Flags to Avoid

### Scam Directories
**Beware of:**
- Directories asking for $500+ with no track record
- "Featured listing" upsells (usually low ROI)
- Directories with no traffic (check Similarweb.com)
- Directories that auto-create listings and charge to claim them

**Rule:** Only pay for established directories with proven traffic (Psychology Today, TherapyDen)

### Bad Listings
- Duplicate listings (confuses Google)
- Inactive/abandoned listings with wrong info
- Spammy directories with low quality

**Action:** Regularly search for your business name and claim/remove bad listings
