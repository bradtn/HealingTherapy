How to Get Your Child Evaluated for Autism in Michigan: Insurance & Process Guide

If you're a parent in Michigan wondering whether your child might be on the autism spectrum, you're not alone. Many families in Dearborn, Detroit, and throughout Wayne and Oakland counties have the same questions: How do I get my child evaluated? Will my insurance cover it? What should I expect?

At Healing Therapy Center, we provide comprehensive autism evaluations for children and adults in Metro Detroit. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know about the evaluation process and how to use your insurance benefits.

Signs Your Child May Need an Autism Evaluation

Every child develops differently, but certain patterns may indicate it's time to seek a professional evaluation. Consider scheduling an assessment if your child shows:

  • Social differences: Limited eye contact, difficulty making friends, or seeming uninterested in other children
  • Communication challenges: Delayed speech, repeating words or phrases, or difficulty with back-and-forth conversation
  • Repetitive behaviors: Hand flapping, rocking, lining up toys, or intense focus on specific interests
  • Sensory sensitivities: Strong reactions to sounds, textures, lights, or certain foods
  • Routine dependence: Significant distress when routines change

If you're noticing these signs, our free online autism screening tool can help you determine if a full evaluation is recommended.

What Happens During an Autism Evaluation?

A comprehensive autism evaluation at our Dearborn clinic typically includes several components:

1. Parent/Caregiver Interview

We'll gather detailed information about your child's developmental history, current behaviors, and your specific concerns. This helps us understand your child's unique profile.

2. Direct Observation and Assessment

Our licensed psychologists use gold-standard assessment tools, including the ADOS-2 (Autism Diagnostic Observation Schedule), to observe your child's social communication, play, and behavior patterns.

3. Cognitive and Developmental Testing

We assess your child's intellectual functioning, language abilities, and adaptive skills to get a complete picture of their strengths and needs.

4. Feedback Session

You'll receive a thorough explanation of our findings, a formal diagnosis (if applicable), and personalized recommendations for therapy, school accommodations, and support services.

5. Written Report

We provide a comprehensive written report that you can share with schools, therapists, and other providers to help your child get the support they need.

Insurance Coverage for Autism Evaluations in Michigan

One of the most common questions we receive is about insurance coverage. The good news is that most major insurance plans in Michigan cover autism evaluations as part of their mental health or diagnostic benefits.

Insurance Plans We Accept

Healing Therapy Center is in-network with many of Michigan's largest insurance providers:

  • Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan (BCBS)
  • Aetna
  • McLaren Health Plan
  • Priority Health
  • HAP (Health Alliance Plan)

Understanding Your Coverage

Your out-of-pocket cost for an autism evaluation depends on your specific plan's:

  • Deductible: The amount you pay before insurance kicks in
  • Copay: A fixed amount you pay per visit
  • Coinsurance: A percentage of the cost you're responsible for
  • Out-of-pocket maximum: The most you'll pay in a year

Questions to Ask Your Insurance Company

If you want to contact your insurance directly, here are the key questions to ask:

  1. "Does my plan cover psychological testing or autism evaluations?"
  2. "Is Healing Therapy Center in Dearborn in-network?"
  3. "What is my deductible, and how much have I met this year?"
  4. "What is my copay or coinsurance for outpatient mental health services?"
  5. "Do I need a referral or prior authorization?"

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an autism evaluation take?

A comprehensive evaluation typically takes 3-6 hours, sometimes spread across 2-3 appointments. This allows us to gather thorough information and provide an accurate assessment.

At what age can my child be evaluated?

Children can be reliably evaluated for autism as early as 18-24 months. However, we conduct evaluations for children, teens, and adults of all ages. It's never too late to get answers.

What if my child doesn't have autism?

An evaluation provides valuable information regardless of the outcome. If your child doesn't meet criteria for autism, we often identify other factors that may be affecting their development and can recommend appropriate support.

How soon can I get an appointment?

We understand that waiting is difficult when you have concerns about your child. Call us at (313) 654-1915 to discuss availability and get scheduled.
